# Changelog

Notable changes to the Bulwark mod are recorded here. Entries remain under
`[Unreleased]` until they are included in a published release.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

#### 2026-10-01

- Increased Thorn Totem's maximum summon limit at mastery rank 5 from 4 to 5, matching its description.
- Defined Echoing Assault's 15% raw attack damage reduction once at the base rank; upgrades continue to increase echo damage.
- Increased Barbed Carapace's Strength-to-Thorns conversion from 0.25 to 0.33 Thorns per Strength.
- Reduced Ruthless Carapace's pulse radius from 120 to 90.
- Reduced Ruthless Carapace pulse damage from 100% to 50% of its Thorns value.
- Reduced Retribution's Thorns gain to 10/20/30/40/50 and the Health-to-Thorns conversion to 1/2/3/4/5% by rank.
- Reduced Barbed Lash's mana cost to 12/14/16/18/20 by rank.
- Restored Bulwark's own title and matched the Warrior title bonus format: grants 3 Health per hero level, with the displayed total scaling as hero level × 3.
