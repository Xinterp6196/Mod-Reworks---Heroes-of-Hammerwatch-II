# Changelog

Notable changes to the Bulwark mod are recorded here. Entries remain under
`[Unreleased]` until they are included in a published release.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

#### 2026-10-01

- Increased Thorn Totem's maximum summon limit at mastery rank 5 from 4 to 5, matching its description.
- Standardized Echoing Assault's raw attack damage reduction at 15% across all ranks; echo damage continues to increase with rank.
- Increased Barbed Carapace's Strength-to-Thorns conversion from 0.25 to 0.33 Thorns per Strength.
- Reduced Ruthless Carapace's pulse radius from 120 to 90.
